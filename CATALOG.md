# Repertoire catalog

Harmonic Lab includes **68 library selections** and **Stairway to Tuning** as a dedicated featured workspace. Every entry is available on standard guitar, drop-D guitar, high-G ukulele, low-G ukulele, and baritone ukulele. Register fitting and chord voicing are reported separately from key transposition.

The scope column distinguishes themes, opening patterns, melodies, and complete exercises. Suggested tablature is an educational arrangement.
